# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SpiritVale players who want a compact, glanceable view of base and job XP while actively playing. The overlay must stay readable without competing with combat or the game's own interface.

## Product Purpose

Provide trustworthy, locally observed XP totals and conservative source attribution in a movable in-game meter. Success means players can understand session progress at a glance and configure the display without leaving the game's established UI patterns.

## Brand Personality

Compact, game-native, dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid web-dashboard chrome, attention-seeking overlays, speculative data, low-contrast decoration, and visual treatments that feel detached from SpiritVale's interface.

## Design Principles

- Fit the game: reuse SpiritVale's typography, panel treatment, and interaction conventions.
- Preserve focus: keep the overlay compact and visually subordinate to gameplay.
- Make data trustworthy: distinguish authoritative totals from conservative attribution.
- Keep control direct: dragging, resizing, and configuration should be immediate and predictable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain clear contrast, readable text at supported scales, non-color-only data labels, and a static presentation that does not depend on animation.
